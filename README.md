# From Classical to Quantum

## 📌 Event Overview

The **From Classical to Quantum** seminar-cum-workshop was conducted over two days, on **13 and 14 November 2025**, to introduce students to the transition from classical computing to quantum technology.

The event consisted of an online expert seminar on the first day, followed by a hands-on workshop on the second day. The sessions focused on quantum technology, Machine Learning (ML), and Quantum Machine Learning (QML), providing students with insights into emerging technologies and their real-world applications.

## 📅 Event Details

### Day 1: Online Seminar

- **Date:** 13 November 2025
- **Mode:** Online
- **Platform:** Google Meet
- **Speaker:** Dr. Manish K. Gupta
- **Affiliation:** IIT Kanpur, Kaushalya University
- **Topic:** Transition from Classical Computing to Quantum Technology

The first day featured an expert seminar introducing students to the evolution of computing, the fundamental differences between classical computing and quantum technology, and the growing importance of quantum-based approaches in modern technological research.

### Day 2: Hands-on Workshop

- **Date:** 14 November 2025
- **Time:** 2:30 PM
- **Venue:** Room A207
- **Speaker:** Mr. Britant
- **Affiliation:** CDAC India, National Quantum Mission
- **Topics:** Machine Learning (ML) and Quantum Machine Learning (QML)

The second day featured a hands-on workshop focused on Machine Learning and Quantum Machine Learning. The session helped students explore these concepts, understand their applications, and gain practical exposure to emerging computational technologies.

## 🎯 Objectives of the Event

The primary objective of the **From Classical to Quantum** seminar-cum-workshop was to help students understand the evolution of computing from traditional classical systems to modern quantum technologies.

The event aimed to:

- Introduce students to the fundamental differences between classical computing and quantum technology.
- Provide an understanding of Machine Learning (ML) and Quantum Machine Learning (QML).
- Offer practical exposure through an interactive, hands-on workshop.
- Facilitate knowledge sharing through expert-led sessions.
- Help students explore real-world applications of emerging technologies.
- Encourage technical curiosity, analytical thinking, and problem-solving skills.
- Create awareness of future academic and career opportunities in quantum computing, artificial intelligence, and machine learning.

## 🎓 Learning Outcomes and Benefits

The event provided students with valuable theoretical knowledge and practical exposure to emerging computing technologies.

The major learning outcomes included:

- **Understanding Quantum Technology:** Students learned about the fundamental differences between classical computing and quantum technology.
- **Machine Learning Skills:** Participants gained exposure to Machine Learning concepts and their applications.
- **Introduction to Quantum Machine Learning:** Students explored the emerging field of QML and its potential applications.
- **Practical Experience:** The hands-on workshop provided opportunities to engage with modern technological concepts and tools.
- **Problem-Solving and Critical Thinking:** The sessions encouraged analytical thinking and exploration of innovative approaches to computational problems.
- **Expert Interaction:** Students had the opportunity to learn from professionals and gain insights into real-world technological applications.
- **Career Awareness:** The event encouraged participants to explore future opportunities in quantum computing, artificial intelligence, and machine learning.

Overall, the event strengthened students' technical understanding, encouraged them to explore innovative ideas, and inspired them to learn more about the future of computing.
